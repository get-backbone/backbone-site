How it works

  1. Licence - purchase a Backbone platform licence file, see Pricing below.
  2. Fork - your organization is added as a Contributor, so you can fork the backbone-platform repository into your own GitHub org account.
  3. Configure - set your domain, secrets, High Availability and FinOps requirements.
  4. Provision - launch CI/CD workflows into your GitHub account; full release process and infrastructure automation.
  5. Deploy - CI/CD ships it to ECS with security and observability already wired in. Deployed into your AWS accounts.
  6. Own it - your code, your infrastructure, your data. One licence, unlimited environments.
