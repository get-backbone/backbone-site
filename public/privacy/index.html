Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit the Backbone website (the “Website”).

1. Information we collect

The Website is primarily informational. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the Website. However, if you submit a form on the Website, we collect the information you provide (for example, your email address).

2. How we use information

If you provide contact information, we use it to respond to your request, follow up about Backbone, and communicate with you about your inquiry.

3. Cookies and analytics

We use Page Pulse to understand Website traffic and engagement (for example, page views, clicks, and basic performance metrics). Page Pulse stores anonymous visitor and session identifiers in browser local storage / session storage; we do not use advertising cookies. See getpagepulse.io for provider details.

4. Third-party services

The Website may rely on third-party providers to deliver specific functions (for example, email form handling, scheduling, and payment processing). When you interact with those features, information may be processed by those providers under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data retention

We retain information submitted through the Website only as long as needed to address your inquiry and maintain reasonable business records, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through the Website. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact hello@backbonehq.io.
