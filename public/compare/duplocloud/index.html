What you buy

DuploCloud automates DevOps in your AWS, Azure, or GCP account: provisioning, RBAC, CI/CD hooks, and compliance mapped to SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, and GDPR - with audit trails. It's well funded, an AWS Premier Tier Partner, and sells governance in your cloud from day one.

Backbone is a licensed, forkable codebase of running app services plus delivery, zero-trust, observability, and mapped compliance evidence - in your AWS account. You own the code, infra, and data. You can read the decisions.

Real overlap

Both run in your account. Both sell day-one governance without a multi-year DIY build. If DuploCloud comes up on a call, treat it as a serious alternative - not adjacent noise.

Where it splits

DuploCloud orchestrates cloud config via an agent and control plane. It doesn't ship auth, users, notifications, documents, or BFF as code you fork. It isn't a "read the ADRs" app-runtime story.

Backbone's center is the app-services layer and the operating model around it. Need identity, audit, documents, notifications, plus the rails? That is Backbone. Need to automate and govern infra you already design? DuploCloud is closer.

When DuploCloud wins

You already know how to build the product layer. The hard problem is governing and automating cloud ops across accounts with compliance evidence. That is DuploCloud. Backbone is for teams that still need the runnable product foundation - not only the DevOps control plane around it.

Honest caveats

  • DuploCloud is closer to a head-to-head than anything else on our compare page. We're not pretending otherwise.
  • "Your AWS + compliance" overlap isn't feature parity. App runtime is Backbone's center; infra automation is DuploCloud's.
  • Some companies will use both over time. Day one for a SaaS product without a platform team is usually the foundation question first.
