What you buy

Coherence automates CI/CD, ephemeral previews, and infra provisioning in your AWS or GCP account. It's delivery and env tooling with PaaS speed, without a vendor-hosted data plane.

Backbone includes a delivery spine, plus app services, zero-trust, observability, and governance evidence - one forkable operating model in your AWS account.

Real overlap

Both ship into your cloud with CI/CD and env automation. Buyers are often founders who rejected multi-tenant PaaS but still want that speed.

Where it splits

Coherence doesn't sell auth, audit, documents, notifications, or packaged zero-trust / compliance - delivery is the product. Backbone's delivery layer is one slice of a broader foundation: Render-like speed, but in your account, with the app layer included.

When Coherence wins

Your hard problem is environments, pipelines, and provisioning - and you already have (or will build) auth, audit, and app services. Coherence is a strong delivery answer. Backbone is for teams that need that layer and the runnable product foundation around it.

Honest caveats

  • This is a delivery-layer comparison. Not every buyer is choosing between the two.
  • Closer to Backbone than Render on ownership (your cloud). Farther on scope (no app-services foundation).
  • Coherence-like delivery plus point-solution auth is a valid DIY path - with the usual seam costs.
